OUR APPROACH

WHO WE
ARE / ABOUT US

Built on Experience. Designed for What’s Next.


SkyArc Projects is a Design & Build, Interior Fit-Out, Civil Works and Project Management company delivering spaces from initial concept through to final handover.

We bring together architecture, engineering, procurement, commercial management and on-site execution under one coordinated delivery structure — giving our clients a single point of responsibility throughout the project lifecycle.

Our foundations are built on more than a decade of practical design and construction experience, including 150+ residential and interior projects delivered through our legacy SmartNest practice. SkyArc represents the next stage of that journey, expanding this experience into larger and more complex residential, commercial, clubhouse, hospitality and institutional projects.

Our approach combines design with disciplined project execution. Every project is managed around clear programmes, defined budgets, quality controls, transparent procurement and strong site supervision — with safety, accountability and client satisfaction at the centre of how we work.
